# v0.4.5

- New launcher design: the world's artwork now fills the whole window in full colour, with a slim icon rail on the left that expands when you hover it, and a single Launch button in the bottom-right corner.
- Spec chips (screenshots, Minecraft, Forge, settings, Starlight) sit in one tidy row at the top right, and every control uses the same smoked-glass look with a faint hairline.
- Buttons animate on hover and press: the Launch icon spins, the update icon turns, settings and Starlight rotate, and the rail opens smoothly. Reduced-motion is respected.
- Settings opens as a rounded sheet that scrolls to the bug-report section, and the window-size picker matches the new theme.
